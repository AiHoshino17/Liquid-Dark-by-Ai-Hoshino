                        AddCoverage > AddHair > Tidy > TrueTone > AddMissing > Tidy > AddVivid > Relocate > AddEyeRings (LAST)
 tintPaletteTidy        NEW. cost rule share x distance, merges look-alikes into a bigger same-hue colour. Protects eye, vivid,
                        blush, hair >= 3 %, rank 1. Skips palettes under 3 colours.
 tintPaletteGiveSlot    NEW. frees one slot (smallest plain colour) for a new detail colour.
 tintPaletteAddVivid    NEW. every hue family with >= 0.6 % vivid pixels (chroma >= .085) must be in the palette; a duller colour of
                        that hue is replaced by the true vivid colour (top 30 % chroma). Flags vivid.
 tintPaletteAddEyeRings NEW. highlight on a dark pupil + eye-white nearby + unrepresented iris colour around it; prefers a pair;
                        skips text/labels (crowd rule) and skin pixels; adopts an existing palette colour as the eye if it matches.
 tintPaletteGrad        CHANGED: vivid colours (< 8 %) are drawn as accents on top; vivid / eye glows are kept above y = 56 % so
                        the dark bottom veil does not hide them.
